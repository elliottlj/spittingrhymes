## About this game

Type words that rhyme with the target word before time runs out. **True rhymes** (+10) sound identical from the stressed vowel onward. **Slant rhymes** (+2) share only the vowel sound or only the ending. Everything is checked against the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (~126,000 words), so some accepted rhymes may be obscure.

## FAQ

### Why wasn't my word accepted?

Either it doesn't rhyme by the true/slant rules above, or it isn't in the dictionary — which leans American English and doesn't include every proper noun.

### Can I share a round with someone?

Yes — once a round ends, hit "Copy link". It encodes both the target word and the timer length, so whoever opens it plays the exact same round.

### Is my stats history saved anywhere online?

No. It's stored only in your browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

### Can I clear my history?

Yes, the trash icon in the Stats panel clears it. This can't be undone.
